# Data Analytics Portfolio

A collection of data analytics projects demonstrating skills in statistical analysis, customer behaviour analytics, and editorial data analysis using Python, pandas, and matplotlib.

> **Note:** GitHub's notebook preview has a known rendering limitation. Use the nbviewer links below to view the fully rendered notebooks.
>
> ## Projects
>
> ### 1. A/B Test Analysis: Subscription Page Conversion Optimisation
> Tests whether a redesigned subscription landing page improves conversion rate using a two-proportion z-test, chi-square validation, confidence intervals, and power analysis.
>
> **[View rendered notebook](https://nbviewer.org/github/ao-chaos/data-analytics-portfolio/blob/main/ab_test_analysis.ipynb)**
>
> **Key skills:** Hypothesis testing, statistical significance, confidence intervals, power analysis, business impact estimation
>
> ---
>
> ### 2. Cohort Retention Analysis: Digital Subscription Service
> Tracks 12 monthly subscriber cohorts through their lifecycle, producing a retention heatmap, churn rate analysis, and customer lifetime value (LTV) estimates.
>
> **[View rendered notebook](https://nbviewer.org/github/ao-chaos/data-analytics-portfolio/blob/main/cohort_retention_analysis.ipynb)**
>
> **Key skills:** Cohort analysis, retention curves, churn modelling, LTV calculation, trend analysis
>
> ---
>
> ### 3. News Article Engagement Analysis
> Analyses content performance across sections, publication timing, article length, and topics for a digital news platform.
>
> **[View rendered notebook](https://nbviewer.org/github/ao-chaos/data-analytics-portfolio/blob/main/news_engagement_analysis.ipynb)**
>
> **Key skills:** Exploratory data analysis, correlation analysis, editorial analytics, data visualisation
>
> ---
>
